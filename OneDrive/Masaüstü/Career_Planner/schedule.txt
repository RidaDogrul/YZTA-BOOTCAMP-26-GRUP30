**Week 1 (Start Date: 2025-12-20)**

Tasks:
1. Research and select an online bachelor's degree program in Computer Science, Mathematics, Information Technology or a related field. 
2. Begin introductory courses in your chosen degree program.

Explanation: The first week is all about setting your educational foundation. Choose a degree program that suits your needs and start with the basics.

**Week 2 (Start Date: 2025-12-27)**

Tasks:
1. Continue with your degree program courses.
2. Start a basic Python programming course online.

Explanation: In addition to your degree courses, start learning Python programming, a key language in AI engineering.

**Week 3 (Start Date: 2026-01-03)**

Tasks:
1. Continue with your degree program and Python courses.
2. Begin a basic course in Java programming online.

Explanation: Adding Java to your programming skills will broaden your capabilities and make you more versatile in the AI field.

**Week 4 (Start Date: 2026-01-10)**

Tasks:
1. Maintain progress in your degree program, Python, and Java courses.
2. Start a basic course in R programming online.

Explanation: R programming is another essential skill for AI engineers. This week, add it to your learning schedule.

**Week 5 (Start Date: 2026-01-17)**

Tasks:
1. Keep up with your degree program and programming courses.
2. Begin an introductory course in AI and Machine Learning on a platform like Coursera, edX, or Udacity.

Explanation: Now that you're getting comfortable with programming, it's time to dive into AI and Machine Learning. This will give you a basic understanding of these areas.

**Week 6 (Start Date: 2026-01-24)**

Tasks:
1. Continue with your degree program, programming, and AI courses.
2. Research Master's degree or specialized certification programs in AI or Machine Learning.
3. Start a personal project to apply what you've learned so far.

Explanation: In the final week, start looking into further education options while also applying your new skills in a practical project. This will help you gain experience and understand how the concepts you've learned work in action.

Remember, this is just a 6-week plan to get you started. Becoming an AI engineer requires continuous learning and practice. Stay motivated, keep learning, and you'll be on the path to an exciting career in AI. Good luck!
